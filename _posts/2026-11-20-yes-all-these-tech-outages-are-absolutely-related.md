Outages have hit much of the country in recent weeks. Microsoft 365 was degraded or down for days, and the FAA had its longest nationwide ground stop since 9/11. Chase, Wells Fargo, Amtrak, Apple, and the NSA were affected too, and “cyberattack” became a top Google search term as people wondered whether the incidents were connected.

My answer: these incidents are absolutely related. I doubt a cyberattack is the cause, beyond the usual activity from foreign adversaries. Instead, I tie them indirectly to the AI hype cycle: executives making poor decisions after buying into AI promises, government officials weakening security because of the hype, and software engineers losing their edge.
