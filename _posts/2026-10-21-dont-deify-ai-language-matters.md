My talk from BarCamp Philly 2026 is a plea to reconsider how we talk about “AI”. It’s not a great term, since LLMs are neither artificial nor intelligent, but unfortunately we’re stuck with it, unless we want to adopt something even dumber, like “SI”: “Super Intelligence!” “AI” is a marketing term, not a technical term.

I believe we should start calling out those who are fueling this hype cycle with dubious motives. We need to stop adopting the language tech bros are forcing and the media is parroting. There’s been way too much “CEO SAID A THING” journalism, and “AI” has sucked the oxygen out of the room for other important topics. It feels like mistakes we’ve made as a society are being repeated, on steroids.
