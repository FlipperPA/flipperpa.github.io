After over four decades of coding, I’ve come to the conclusion that it is a miracle that anything I write works. This talk pokes fun at some of the absurdity of what we do, celebrates our triumphs over impostor syndrome, and explores how our projects can learn from another very successful anarchy: recovery groups around the world.

The talk was presented at [DjangoCon US 2022](https://2022.djangocon.us/talks/miracles-in-anarchy/).
