Some pivotal moments of my life have come at BarCamp Philly, a one-day unconference about creativity, design, and development in Philadelphia. I got involved as a volunteer in 2009, and later helped organize the event and host it at Wharton.

After several years of pandemic hiatus, the organization team is hoping to host BarCamp Philly again this fall. The event began in 2008 and last ran virtually in 2020.
