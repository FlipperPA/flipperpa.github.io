Marlene and I both gave talks on "A.I." at the DjangoCon US 2025 conference, but with very different angles. In this episode, the four of us discuss the real-world strengths and weaknesses of the emerging technology, how it is impacting developers' daily workflows, and also examples of failures.

While we come at this technology from different angles and use it in different ways, we find common ground of agreement in where it might be headed.
