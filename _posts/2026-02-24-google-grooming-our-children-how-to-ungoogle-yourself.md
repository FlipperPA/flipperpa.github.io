We just celebrated our child’s birthday, and without our consent, Google decided it knows what’s best for her.

Google has begun emailing children who are about to turn 13 to let them know they can drop their parental controls, and the parents aren’t told. It’s the newest, and possibly most alarming, step in a long-running trend.

For decades, Google has intruded on our privacy while grooming all of us to be its unwitting data servants. Regaining control, and some privacy, is simpler than most people assume.
