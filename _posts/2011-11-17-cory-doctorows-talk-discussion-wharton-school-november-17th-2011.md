Cory's Biography: [Cory Doctorow](http://craphound.com/) is a science fiction author, activist, journalist and blogger — the co-editor of [Boing Boing](https://boingboing.net/) and the author of Tor Teens/HarperCollins UK novels like FOR THE WIN and the bestselling LITTLE BROTHER. He is the former European director of the Electronic Frontier Foundation and co-founded the UK Open Rights Group. Born in Toronto, Canada, he now lives in London.
