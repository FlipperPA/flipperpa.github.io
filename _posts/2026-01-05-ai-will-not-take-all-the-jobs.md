You’ve seen the headlines: story after story predicting doom for the job market.

Some people treat Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei’s claim that AI will eliminate half of all entry-level white collar jobs within five years as settled fact, and commentators are already debating what comes next for the displaced workers. The most extreme forecast I’ve seen comes from University of Louisville associate professor Roman Yampolskiy, who predicts AI will replace 99% of work by 2030.

History tells a very different story.
