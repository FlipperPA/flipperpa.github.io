I originally wrote this song while in a band called Speed Union 40 in 1994. The lead singer and I had an intense and creative friendship that fell apart, largely because of the different directions our lives were headed. After an acrimonious breakup, I rewrote the lyrics to be about our time together, and what I imagined our futures held.
