It is crucial to have people with deep technical understanding at the highest levels of leadership, yet big tech firms around the world increasingly shy away from that expertise, sometimes with calamitous consequences.

That isn’t to say leadership should be made up entirely of engineers who’ve risen through the ranks. But a technology-centered company that pushes technical knowledge out of its top roles is typically in trouble.

Tech firms run solely by non-technical leaders tend to be penny wise and dollar stupid, turning innovative, experimental cultures into cautious ones focused on quarterly results.
